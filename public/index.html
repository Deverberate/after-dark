02 The Machine SEC // 02

The flagship wears black.

MacBook Pro 16‑inch. M4 Max. The top of the line, in the only finish that makes sense here.

The 16‑inch M4 Max is not a cautious upgrade. It is a desktop-class machine that happens to fold: twelve performance cores, four efficiency cores, up to forty GPU cores, and a memory subsystem wide enough to keep all of them fed.

  • 01

    A display that ends arguments

    16.2‑inch Liquid Retina XDR. 3456 × 2234 at 254 ppi. ProMotion up to 120 Hz. 1,000 nits sustained, 1,600 nits peak HDR, and an optional nano-texture finish that turns hard light into matte grey.

  • 02

    Enough battery to outlast the night

    Up to 21 hours of video playback on the 16‑inch M4 Max. Enough to finish the cut, the build, or the render without hunting for a wall socket.

  • 03

    Ports with somewhere to be

    Three Thunderbolt 5 ports at up to 120 Gb/s, HDMI, an SDXC slot, MagSafe 3, and a headphone jack. The adapter dongle stays in the drawer.

  • 04

    A room that hears you

    A 12 MP Center Stage camera with Desk View, a six-speaker system with force-cancelling woofers, Spatial Audio, and a three-mic array. The call is the only thing that breaks the illusion.

“The city doesn’t sleep. Neither does the render queue.”
